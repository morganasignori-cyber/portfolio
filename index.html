Case Study · Open Innovation

Apple Card &
Goldman Sachs

Context

In 2019, Apple and Goldman Sachs launched Apple Card — a titanium credit card fully integrated into the iPhone ecosystem. This partnership is a landmark case of open innovation: two companies from entirely different industries co-creating a product neither could have built alone.

Apple contributed its design philosophy, consumer trust, and distribution through Apple Wallet. Goldman Sachs brought banking infrastructure, regulatory expertise, and lending capabilities — entering consumer finance for the first time in its 150-year history.

Open Innovation Lens

The case illustrates Chesbrough’s open innovation model: Apple leveraged Goldman’s external capabilities rather than building a banking license from scratch. Conversely, Goldman gained direct consumer access it had never achieved on its own.

Key dimensions include co-branding strategy, data-sharing architecture, UX-first product design, and ecosystem lock-in through Daily Cash rewards tied exclusively to Apple Pay.

2026 · Thesis · Sustainability

Sustainability & Brand Loyalty
— Promasidor

Project Overview

This thesis explores how sustainability practices in emerging markets can influence consumer satisfaction and brand loyalty. The research is conducted in collaboration with Promasidor, an African food company operating in multiple developing markets.

The project focuses on understanding how responsible business practices translate into perceived value, trust, and long-term consumer relationships.

Approach

The research combines academic frameworks on sustainability and consumer behavior with real-world insights from the food industry in emerging markets.

It analyzes how sustainability initiatives impact brand perception and how these effects vary across different market contexts.

Key Focus

• Sustainability as a driver of brand trust • Consumer perception in emerging markets • Link between ethical practices and loyalty
